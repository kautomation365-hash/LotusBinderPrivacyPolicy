The Application may automatically collect limited diagnostic and usage information, such as device type, operating system version, and general application usage statistics, in order to improve the Application and its features.

The Application may use on-device image analysis technologies to recognize and organize card information. Image data is processed locally on your device and is not uploaded to external servers by the Application.
